# Phase 1 (MVP) – Preventive Healthcare + Hospital Management Platform

## Core Vision

A unified healthcare ecosystem where **Patients, Doctors, Labs, Hospitals, and Healthcare Staff** operate on a single patient health record using a **Unique Patient Health ID**.

# 3. Health Score Engine

Every patient receives a Health Score (0–100).

### Inputs

* BMI
* Existing Conditions
* Lab Reports
* Lifestyle Factors
* Risk Factors

### Categories

* 80–100 → Healthy
* 60–79 → Moderate Risk
* 40–59 → High Risk
* Below 40 → Critical

Used for:

* Doctor Prioritization
* Hospital Alerts
* Preventive Care Monitoring

# Phase 1 Deliverable

A complete **Preventive Healthcare Platform + Electronic Health Record (EHR) + Hospital Management System (HMS)** featuring:

* Unified Patient Health ID
* Preventive Health Scoring
* Doctor Workflow Automation
* AI-Assisted Consultations
* Lab Integration
* Hospital Operations Management
* Staff & Ward Management
* Centralized Longitudinal Health Records

This is a strong MVP that can be piloted with clinics, diagnostic labs, and small-to-medium hospitals while already offering significant differentiation from typical telemedicine apps.
